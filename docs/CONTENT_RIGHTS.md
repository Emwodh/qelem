## 2. Risk Mitigation & Compliance Mandate

1. **No Commercial Resale of Raw Papers:** Qelem does not sell raw PDF files or unaugmented government papers. Value is provided through interactive testing, timing telemetry, and original pedagogical derivations.
2. **Takedown & Cooperative Protocol:** If the Educational Assessment and Examinations Service (EAES) or Ministry of Education requests modifications or restricts digital distribution of specific examination years, Qelem engineering maintains the capability to unpublish questions via the administrative portal within 2 hours.
3. **Formal Engagement:** The formal request letter (`docs/DRAFT_EAES_PERMISSION_LETTER.md`) must be reviewed and submitted to the EAES Directorate to establish official public-private academic partnership.
