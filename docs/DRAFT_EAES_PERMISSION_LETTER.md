We write to your esteemed office on behalf of **Qelem (ቀለም)**, an Ethiopian educational technology initiative committed to expanding academic accessibility and computer-based test (CBT) readiness for secondary school students preparing for the national Ethiopian University Entrance Examinations (EUEE).

### 1. Purpose & Educational Mission
With the national transition toward digital and computer-based examination administration by the EAES, secondary school candidates across both urban and regional test centers require authentic digital practice environments. Many students currently struggle to access organized past examination papers with verified, step-by-step mathematical proofs and conceptual explanations.

Qelem has developed a local-first, low-bandwidth, and offline-resilient web platform designed to provide:
- **Realistic Computer-Based Testing (CBT) Simulations:** Accurately mirroring national exam durations, pacing conditioning, and question navigation.
- **STEM Formula Accessibility:** Utilizing high-performance KaTeX typesetting for complex mathematical and physical equations.
- **Pedagogical Remediation:** Interactive mistake tracking and step-by-step derivations authored by Ethiopian secondary school educators.

### 2. Specific Scope of Requested Authorization
We respectfully request formal, non-exclusive permission from the Educational Assessment and Examinations Service to:
1. Digitize, categorize, and archive historical EUEE national examination question papers from academic years **2005 E.C. through 2016 E.C.** across Natural and Social Science streams.
2. Present these past examination questions in digital practice and mock test formats for registered Ethiopian secondary school candidates.
3. Augment each question with original step-by-step derivations, pedagogical explanations, and distractor analyses prepared by our academic team.

### 3. Commitment to Attribution, Security & Non-Exclusivity
Qelem explicitly affirms:
- **Full Attribution:** All official examination questions and syllabi structures will be prominently attributed to the FDRE Ministry of Education and the Educational Assessment and Examinations Service.
- **No Claims of Ownership:** Qelem will make no claim of copyright or intellectual property over official examination questions, official national scoring keys, or government curriculum frameworks.
- **Data Protection & Compliance:** The platform adheres to strict data privacy standards and commits to complying immediately with any curriculum directives, question revisions, or policy guidance issued by your office.
- **Equal Access & Free Practice:** Foundational past examination questions and diagnostic tests will remain accessible to all Ethiopian candidates regardless of subscription status.
