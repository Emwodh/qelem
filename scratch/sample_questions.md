1. A mass of $m = 2\,\text{kg}$ is attached to a vertical spring with spring constant $k = 200\,\text{N/m}$. What is the period of oscillation?
A) $0.63\,\text{s}$
B) $1.26\,\text{s}$
C) $3.14\,\text{s}$
D) $0.20\,\text{s}$
Answer: A
Explanation: The period of a mass-spring harmonic oscillator is given by:
$$T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}$$
Substituting the given values:
$$T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{2}{200}} = 2\pi\sqrt{0.01} = 2\pi(0.1) \approx 0.628\,\text{s}$$

### Question 2:
Which of the following is equivalent to the electrostatic force between two point charges $q_1$ and $q_2$ separated by a distance $r$?
- **A)** $F = k_e \frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2}$
- **B)** $F = k_e \frac{q_1 q_2}{r}$
- **C)** $F = k_e \frac{q_1^2}{r^2}$
- **D)** $F = \frac{1}{r^2}$
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** According to Coulomb's Law, the electrostatic force between two static charges is directly proportional to the product of charges and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.
$$F = k_e \frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}$$
